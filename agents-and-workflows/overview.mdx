Agents & Workflows is where you turn detection and track data from your computer vision models into real business logic, defining when events get created and posted in Worlds. There are two ways to do it, and they work side by side:

- **Agents** - Iris builds an agent with you from a conversation or a document, then runs it on a schedule, or whenever something happens, to watch your cameras and data and report what it finds.
- **Workflows** - you build the logic yourself in Worlds' embedded n8n instance, and post the events it creates under a **chronicle producer**.

## Before You Start

- Not available to Users. Managers, Administrators, and Super Administrators only.
- Every workflow posts its events under a chronicle producer, so create one before you build a workflow (see Step 1 below). Agents create their own.

## Agents

Agents are built and run with Iris. Tell Iris what you want watched, review the agent she drafts, and activate it, and it runs on its own from then on, posting what it finds to [Event Activity](/monitoring/event-activity) and building dashboards of its results. See the [Agents](/agents-and-workflows/agents) guide for the full walkthrough.

**Note:** If agents aren't enabled in your environment, **Agents** in the navigation opens the Chronicle Producers page described below instead.

## Chronicle Producers

A chronicle producer is a bucket: a category one or more workflows belong to, and where the events those workflows generate get written. Open it from **Agents & Workflows > Chronicle Producers**.

You can theme chronicle producers however makes sense for your organization:

- **By individual use case** - e.g., a "PPE Detection" producer holding a single workflow for validating PPE use, or two workflows, one for capturing and logging valid PPE and another for non-compliance.
- **By larger functional bucket** - e.g., a Productivity producer covering OEE/OLE data and workflows, a Safety producer for all EHS workflows and use cases, and a Security producer.

There's no single right structure, and you can reorganize them later.

Each Iris agent also gets its own chronicle producer, named after the agent, the moment it's activated, so its findings are filed alongside everything else. You'll see those in the list too.

### Step 1: Create a Chronicle Producer

Click **New Chronicle Producer** in the top right. A name and a time zone are required; description, icon, and active/inactive status are optional. The icon appears on every activity the producer posts, so you can tell at a glance where a record came from. Click any existing producer to edit it later, or click **View Events** to see its activities in Event Activity.

Once you're in the Workflow Builder, creating a new workflow includes a setting for which chronicle producer it posts to.

**Warning:** Deleting a chronicle producer permanently removes it along with all of its activities and their events. That includes the producer an Iris agent creates for itself, so deleting an agent's producer deletes everything the agent has found.

## Workflows

Clicking Workflows in the navigation opens Worlds' fully embedded, customized n8n instance in a new tab.

Custom Worlds nodes handle evaluating trigger conditions, creating and posting events, generating event assets, and managing GraphQL subscriptions for live data. On top of that, you get the full native n8n platform: custom coding blocks, third-party integrations, and alerts or notifications routed to Slack, email, and other channels, all from the same canvas.

**Note:** SSO is enabled for n8n across all production environments, so you should land there already logged in. n8n permissions are managed independently from Worlds, though, so you may need an n8n administrator to grant you access to specific workflows you want to edit, clone, or reference.

For general n8n platform basics, see [n8n's website](https://n8n.io/). For how to use Worlds' custom nodes within your n8n instance, see the [Workflows documentation](/getting-started/overview).

## Quick Reference

- **Access** - Manager, Administrator, Super Administrator; not available to Users
- **Agents** - built and run with Iris; see the [Agents](/agents-and-workflows/agents) guide
- **Chronicle Producers** - the bucket a workflow posts to; create one before creating any workflow; name and time zone required, description/icon/active status optional; each Iris agent gets its own
- **Deleting a producer** - permanently removes all of its activities and events, including an agent's findings
- **Workflows** - opens Worlds' embedded n8n instance; custom Worlds nodes plus full native n8n functionality
- **Permissions** - your Worlds role controls access to this section; n8n access itself is managed separately
