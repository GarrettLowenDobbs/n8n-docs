---
title: "3.16.1"
---

November 16, 2026

---

#### **Summaries**

One Chronicle record for a process that plays out across several cameras:

- **Summaries in Event Activity** - A summary ties related activities together into a single journey, like an order moving from the menu board to the pickup window, or a truck moving from the gate to the dock. Set **Type** to **Summary** in Event Activity to list them, then open one for its details, attachments, map, and metadata, plus a timeline that lays out each step in order and plays their video back to back.

---

#### **Agents & Dashboards**

Find the right widget faster, and tell an agent's boards apart at a glance:

- **Searchable Widget Picker** - **Add widget** now opens a picker you can search or browse by category, with a larger preview of each widget before you add it.
- **Agent Dashboard Previews** - An agent's page now shows a preview card for each dashboard it built, grouped into live, drafts to review, and archived versions, so you can spot the one you want without opening each.

---

#### **Overwatch & Builder**

A truer map, and more of your site in Builder:

- **Calibrated Camera View Cones** - Each camera's cone on the map now reflects how far and how wide that camera really sees, measured from its calibration, and stays on the right heading as you rotate the map.
- **GPS Sources in Builder** - **Site Elements** now lists the GPS tracking sources connected to a site under **GPS Sources**, where you can review and rename them separately from cameras.

---

#### **Tracking**

Tracking tuned to each scene, in your hands:

- **Tracker Profile on the Camera Form** - Choose a camera's tracker profile yourself under **Advanced Settings > Tracker profile** when you add or edit a camera or folder. **General** suits most scenes, and **Drive-thru** is tuned for vehicles moving through a lane.

---

#### **System Management**

Tune video and follow jobs without waiting on support:

- **Transcoder and Camera Settings** - Admins can adjust video settings like resolution, frame rate, and privacy masking for a whole edge under **Settings > Edge Deployments**, for one transcoder under **Settings > Transcoders**, or for a single camera under **Camera tuning**.
- **Jobs Timeline** - The **Jobs** page now lays jobs out on a timeline grouped by day, with a count of failures for each day, so it's easier to see what ran, spot a failure, and dig into what went wrong. Each job shows its status, timing, and a step-by-step breakdown, and the page refreshes while jobs run.
